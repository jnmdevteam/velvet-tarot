Velvet Tarot Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · applies to the Velvet Tarot Android app (com.velvetcrow.tarot)

Velvet Tarot is developed and published by JNMDev (“we”, “us”), the developer listed on Google Play. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

Your questions, cards, notes and favourites stay on your phone. There is no account and no developer analytics, and nothing you write is sent to an AI service. The free version shows ads from Google, and Google may process some device data to show and measure those ads, depending on the choices you make.

What stays on your device

  • Readings: your questions, the cards drawn, written pairings you open, your reflections, favourites and journal history.
  • Preferences: theme, effect, text size, sound and motion settings, and your progress toward the styles you can unlock by reading.
  • A sealed note of what you have bought, so paid content works offline. It contains no personal information.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for it. We, the developer, cannot see it.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. To show, measure and protect ads, Google and its partners may collect or receive your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can suggest an approximate location), ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Where the law requires consent, the app asks for it before ads load. You can review your choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Your questions and notes are never included in an ad request. Banner ads sit below browsing pages and a full-screen ad may follow a saved reading. A longer rewarded ad plays only when you choose to watch one to open an extra pairing; you are never required to. Velvet Plus removes all ads.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Google Play handles all payments. The app receives the purchase status from Google Play, checks its signature, and records on your device which items you own. We never receive your card or bank details.

Things you choose to share

  • Images. A shared reading image shows the cards, their short meanings and any pairing you have opened. It never includes your question or reflection. The image goes only to the app you choose.
  • Backups. A journal backup is a file containing your full journal, including questions and notes. It is saved only where you choose to save it.
  • Invitations. Inviting a friend sends a short pre-written message and the Play Store link through the app you choose.
  • Help & feedback. This opens a draft in your own email app, containing your message, the app version, your Android version and your phone model. Nothing is sent until you press send there. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to improve the app. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and delete them when you ask.

Sensors and permissions

Shake to shuffle reads the motion sensor only while the shuffle screen is open. These readings are used for the gesture on your phone and are never stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Your choices

  • Delete single readings, or clear your whole journal, in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently removes everything stored on the device.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices, or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • To ask about, or delete, anything you have emailed us, write to the address below.

US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live in the United States (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah), you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to ask for it to be deleted or corrected, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell your readings, questions or notes. Advertising data is handled by Google as described above. To opt out of ad personalisation, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. For any other request, email the address below; we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

Velvet Tarot is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The app loads only its own bundled files. Ads and purchases are delivered over encrypted connections. Purchase records are protected by a key held in Android's secure keystore.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new effective date.
