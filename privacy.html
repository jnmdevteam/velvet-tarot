Velvet Tarot Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · applies to the Velvet Tarot Android app (com.velvetcrow.tarot)

Velvet Tarot is developed and published by JNMDev (“we”, “us”), the developer listed on Google Play. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

Your questions, cards, notes and favourites stay on your phone. There is no account, and nothing you write is sent to an AI service. Usage stats and crash reports, which are not linked to your name or anything you write, help us improve the app, and you can turn them off in Settings. The free version shows ads from Google, and Google may process some device data to show and measure those ads, depending on the choices you make.

What stays on your device

  • Readings: your questions, the cards drawn, written pairings you open, your reflections, favourites and journal history.
  • Preferences: theme, effect, text size, sound and motion settings, and your progress toward the styles you can unlock by reading.
  • A sealed note of what you have bought, so paid content works offline. It contains no personal information.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for it. We, the developer, cannot see it.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. To show, measure and protect ads, Google and its partners may collect or receive your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can suggest an approximate location), ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Where the law requires consent, the app asks for it before ads load. You can review your choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Your questions and notes are never included in an ad request. Banner ads sit below browsing pages and a full-screen ad may follow a saved reading. A longer rewarded ad plays only when you choose to watch one to open an extra pairing; you are never required to. Velvet Plus removes all ads.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Usage stats and crash reports

To see which features people use and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google. They record things like which screens are opened, which spreads are drawn, which features are used (for example opening connections or choosing a theme), how long the app is used, your device model, Android version, app version, country or region, and an app-instance ID (a random identifier for this installation). Crash reports include technical details about the crash. These services also receive your IP address, which Google uses to estimate country or region. Analytics is configured not to collect your advertising ID and not to use this data for ad personalisation.

Usage stats never include your questions, your notes, the cards you drew or anything you type. This information is used only to understand and improve Velvet Tarot, not to identify you.

You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Turning it off stops collection on your device and resets its analytics ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, usage stats and crash reports stay off unless you agree to them in the consent message the app shows when you first open it; you can change that answer any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Firebase's privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

Google Play handles all payments. The app receives the purchase status from Google Play, checks its signature, and records on your device which items you own. We never receive your card or bank details.

Things you choose to share

  • Images. A shared reading image shows the cards, their short meanings and any pairing you have opened. It never includes your question or reflection. The image goes only to the app you choose.
  • Backups. A journal backup is a file containing your full journal, including questions and notes. It is saved only where you choose to save it.
  • Invitations. Inviting a friend sends a short pre-written message and the Play Store link through the app you choose.
  • Help & feedback. This opens a draft in your own email app, containing your message, the app version, your Android version and your phone model. Nothing is sent until you press send there. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to improve the app. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and delete them when you ask.

Sensors and permissions

Shake to shuffle reads the motion sensor only while the shuffle screen is open. These readings are used for the gesture on your phone and are never stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Your choices

  • Delete single readings, or clear your whole journal, in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently removes everything stored on the device.
  • Turn usage stats and crash reports off in Settings → Share usage stats.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices, or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • To ask about, or delete, anything you have emailed us, write to the address below.

US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live in the United States (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah), you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to ask for it to be deleted or corrected, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell your readings, questions or notes. Advertising data is handled by Google as described above. To opt out of ad personalisation, delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), or use Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app where a choices form is offered for your state. You can also turn off usage stats in the app. For any other request, email the address below; we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Legal bases and your rights (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, JNMDev is the controller for the usage stats and support emails described above, and Google is responsible for its own processing of advertising data. We rely on your consent for personalised advertising (collected through the consent form shown before ads load), and on your consent, given in the same message, for usage stats and crash reports. You can withdraw either at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and switch usage stats off in Settings → Share usage stats. You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Google may process data in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

How long data is kept

  • Everything on your device stays until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Usage stats are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for about 90 days.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Children

Velvet Tarot is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The app's own screens load only files bundled with the app; only ads come from the network. Ads and purchases are delivered over encrypted connections. Purchase records are protected by a key held in Android's secure keystore.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new effective date.
